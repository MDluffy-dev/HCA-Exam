

`/// Birth & Early Life \\\`
 28 Aug 1863
 Perumkatuvila, Venganoor, Thiruvananthapuram
 House Name: Plavatthara House
 Parents: Ayyan & Mala
 Childhood Name: Kali



`/// Kudipallikoodam \\\`
 1905
 Venganoor
 For the education of Pulaya children
 * One of Ayyankali's major efforts for the `educational upliftment` of the oppressed classes.


`/// SJP Sangham \\\`
 Sadhujana Paripalana Sangham
 1907
 By Ayyankali
 Model: SNDP Yogam
 Became `Pulaya Mahasabha` in `1938`
 > Objective:
  * Social emancipation
  * Economic emancipation
  * Educational advancement of Pulayas
 > Mouthpiece:
   Sadhujana Paripalini
    1913
    Publ. from `Sudharshana Press`, Changanassery
    1st Editor: Chembamthara Kalichothi Karuppan



`/// Villuvandi Samaram \\\`
 1893
 > Objective:
   * Right to travel on public roads.
   * Freedom of movement for oppressed castes
 Travelled from `Venganoor → Kowdiar`
 `1st` First major `agitation` led by Ayyankali



`/// Ayyankali and Education Movement \\\`
 Major demand: School admission for Pulaya children
 * Against denial of education to lower-caste children.
 * `Education` became the central weapon of social `upliftment`.


`/// Sreemoolam Praja Sabha \\\`
 Ayyankali `nominated` on 5 Dec 1911
 1st Dalit Member
 1st speech: 27 Feb 1912
 Member for `28` consequtive years
  


`/// Nedumangad Market Riot \\\`
 1912
 Against `denial of entry` to lower castes in `Nedumangad Market`.



`/// Pulaya Lahala \\\`
 1915
 AKA `Uruttambalam Lahala` & `Tonnooramnadu Lahala`
 Objectives:
 * Education for lower-caste children
 * Better wages for workers



`/// Kallumala Agitation \\\`
 1915
 Perinad, Kollam
 AKA `Perinad Lahala`
 Background:
 * LC women were forced to wear:
    `Stone necklaces, Iron ornaments`
   as caste remarks
 End caste-based `dress restrictions`.
 * Ayyankali's Call:
    Reject stone necklaces; Wear ornaments like upper caste
