


`///// CHATTAMBI SWAMIKAL \\\\\`

`// Born \\`
 25 August 1853
 Kannammoola, Thiruvananthapuram
 25 August : `Karunya Dinam` (Benevolence Day) in Kerala.


`// Teachers & Spiritual Life \\`
 Thaikkad Ayya : Hatha Yoga
 CS introduced Thaikkad Ayya to Sree Narayana Guru.

 Subba Jatapadi: 
  Taught Vedas, Upanishads, Sanskrit and Yoga.
  `Encouraged` him to become a `monk`


`// Enlightenment \\`
 Vadiveeswaram (Tamil Nadu)
 `Monastic Name` : Shanmukha Dasan
 Other names:
  Sarva Vidhyadhiraja
  Sribhatarakan
  Sribala Bhatarakan

* SN Guru wrote `Navamanjari` in honour CS


`// Major Works \\`
`Vedadhikara Nirupanam`	    : Vedic learning is for all Hindus
 Pracheena Malayalam	    : Opposes Parasurama Myth
 Moksha Pradeepa Khandanam	: `Criticism of Brahmananda Sivayogi's work (Moksha Pradeepam)`
 Adi Bhasha	                : Important work (against sanskrit)
 Kristhumatha Chedhanam	    : Critique of Christianity
 Keralathile Desa Namangal	: Place name study
 Advaita Chinta Paddhati	: Philosophy
 Nijananda Vilasam	        : Spiritual work
 Jivakarunya Nirupanam	    : Compassion towards living beings


`// Death \\`
 5 May 1924, Panmana Kollam
 > Samadhi:
   Balabhattaraka Temple











`///// PANDIT KARUPPAN \\\\\`

`// Birth \\`
 24 May 1885
 Cheranallur, Ekm
 Araya community
 House name: Sahithya Kudeeram
 `Real Name`: Sankaran
 AKA `Kerala Lincoln`

 * 1st major human rights activist of Cochin State.


`// Literary Works \\`
 1st Poem           : Sthothra Mandaram
 Famous work        : Jathikummi (1st work against caste)
 Aacharabhooshanam  : Against superstition


`// Teacher Appointment Controversy \\`
 1912
 * Appointed teacher in Girls High School, Ernakulam
 * Upper castes opposed his appointment
 * Maharaja supported Karuppan and ended protests.


`// Titles \\`
 > By Cochin Maharaja
   1. Kavithilakan
   2. Sahithya Nipunan
 > By Kerala Varma Valiya Koithampuran
   1. Vidwan (1913)
 > Kerala Lincoln


`// Organizations Founded \\`
 > First Sabha
   Kalyanadayini Sabha, Kodungallur

 > Kochi Pulaya Mahasabha
   1913
   Founder: Pandit Karuppan
   1st President
 
 > Others:
   Araya Samajam,  Vala Samudaya Parishkarini Sabha, Prabodha Chandrodaya Sabha, Vala Seva Samithi (Vaikom)


`// Kayal Sammelanam \\`
 14 February 1913
 Vembanattu Kaayal
 Conference to find a `solution to` the problems faced by the `lower castes`.
 Permission `denied` for Dalit conference.
 Boats tied together.
 Wooden platform created on backwaters.












`///// DR. PALPU \\\\\`
 
 `// Birth \\`
  Padmanabhan Palpu
  1863-1950
  OG name: Kuttiyappi
  `1st medical graduate` from the `Ezhava Community`

  AKA `Political Father of Ezhavas`

  > 1st teacher: Pettayil Raman Pillai Asan


 `// Social Reform Activities \\`
  * `Denied employment` in Travancore Health Services due to caste discrimination.
  * 1st Vice President of SNDP


 `// Ezhava Memorial \\`
  1896
  Petition signed by `13,176 Ezhavas`
  Submitted to  : Sree Moolam Thirunal
  Highlight inequalities in `Travancore society`

 
 `// Ezhava Mahasabha \\`
  1896
  Founder: Dr. Palpu
  * 1st meeting decided to submit Ezhava Memorial


 `// Literary Contribution \\`
  1. Treatment of Theeyas in Travancore
      Written as part of Ezhava Memorial movement










 `///// SAHODARAN AYYAPPAN \\\\\`

 > Birth 
   1889 - 1968
   Cherai, Vypin Island
   Atheist
   `Disciple of SN Guru`

* 1 of the `founding fathers` of `Trade Union Movement in Kerala`


> Sahodara Sangham
  1917
  Destruction of caste system


> Mishrabhojanam
  1917, Cherai
  Inter-caste feast
  People of all castes sat together and ate.
  Organised by : Sahodara Sangam


`// Publications \\`
 1. Sahodaran
     Magazine published from Mattancherry
 
 2. Yukthivadi
     Founder Editor
     Started in 1928
 
 3. Velakkaran
     Newspaper advocating rights of weaker sections


`// Other Organization \\`
 > Vidyaposhini Sabha
   Literary organization.
   Promoted education and intellectual growth.


`// Political Activities \\`
 > Socialist Party
   1938
   Later became `Minister in Cochin Congress Government`.
     

> Famous Slogan
  ജാതി വേണ്ട മതം വേണ്ട ദൈവം വേണ്ട മനുഷ്യന്


> Major Works
  Ahalya,   Ranisandesham,  Parivarthanam
